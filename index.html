Alerts

Checks run only while this app remains open. Browser notifications require permission; no background push service is included.

Free-data limitations: This build uses Alpha Vantage daily time-series data. Prices and volume are end-of-day values, not live or 15-minute delayed quotes. The free service currently publishes a limit of 25 API requests per day; this app uses at most 20 new ticker requests per UTC day to leave a buffer. A 41-ticker watchlist therefore fills over multiple days. The app caches successful history on this device and never invents missing values. This is a curated watchlist scanner, not a complete scan of every NYSE, NASDAQ or OTC listing. Technical scores and trade-planning levels are research calculations, not recommendations or executable orders. No trades are placed.
